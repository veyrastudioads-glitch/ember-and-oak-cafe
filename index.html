Our Heritage

Rooted in coffee.
Built around people.

“We believed that between the relentless pace of modern life and the quiet comfort of a warm mug, there needed to be a physical sanctuary.”

Founded on Market Street in Fort Kochi, Ember & Oak began as an intimate coffee bar with a vintage 2-group espresso machine and an unwavering commitment: honor the farmer, extract with uncompromising precision, and provide a welcoming place where conversations flourish.

We collaborate directly with generational coffee estates in the misty altitudes of Chikmagalur and Wayanad. By paying above fair-trade premiums and roasting in small, meticulous batches, we bring forth the complex nuances of Indian specialty Arabica—from jasmine florality to roasted cacao nibs.
