Sourcing & Terroir

Coffee Origin Notes

All our espresso beverages and pour overs utilize 100% Arabica sourced directly from high-altitude shade-grown estates in the Baba Budan Giri hills of Chikmagalur and the Wayanad plateau. We roast every Tuesday and Friday to guarantee optimal degas and aroma.

Tasting notes rotation: Red Catuai (Plum & Dark Chocolate) · Yellow Bourbon (Honey & Jasmine)
